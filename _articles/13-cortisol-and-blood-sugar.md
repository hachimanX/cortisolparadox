# Cortisol and Blood Sugar: How Stress Hormones Affect Your Energy

Cortisol and blood sugar are closely connected. Raising blood sugar is one of cortisol's main jobs: it makes sure your body has fuel when it needs it. That's useful in the short term. Problems come when cortisol stays very high for a long time, which mostly happens with steroid medicines or a medical condition.

Here's what's well established, and what's less certain for everyday stress.

*This article is for general information and isn't medical advice.*

## Does cortisol raise blood sugar?

Yes. Cortisol belongs to a group of hormones called glucocorticoids, named for their effect on glucose. They raise blood sugar in a few ways. In the liver, they increase the making of new glucose (gluconeogenesis). In muscle and fat, they make cells respond less to insulin, so less glucose is taken up ([Li & Cummins, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9116713/)).

This is part of normal physiology. We cover that daily rhythm in [why cortisol is high in the morning](/guides/why-is-cortisol-high-in-the-morning/).

## When cortisol stays high: steroid medicines and Cushing's

The clearest evidence comes from situations where cortisol, or a cortisol-like medicine, stays high for a long time.

**Steroid medicines.** Glucocorticoid medicines such as prednisone are widely used for conditions like asthma and arthritis. Raised blood sugar is a well-known side effect. A meta-analysis cited in the same review estimated that about a third of people without diabetes who took them for more than a month developed high blood sugar, and about one in five developed diabetes ([Li & Cummins, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9116713/)). If you take these medicines, don't stop them on your own. Talk to your doctor.

**Cushing's syndrome.** In this condition, the body is exposed to too much cortisol over a long time. The NIH lists type 2 diabetes, prediabetes and insulin resistance among its complications ([NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/cushings-syndrome)).

## Everyday stress and blood sugar

This is where the evidence is less clear for healthy people. Short-term stress raises cortisol and adrenaline, which can push blood sugar up for a while. That's expected and usually passes. Whether ordinary, ongoing stress leads to lasting blood sugar problems in otherwise healthy people is much harder to show, because stress also affects sleep, food and activity.

| Claim | What the research shows | How strong |
|---|---|---|
| Cortisol raises blood sugar | Established mechanism: more glucose from the liver, less uptake in muscle and fat ([review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9116713/)) | Strong |
| Steroid medicines raise blood sugar | Common side effect; about a third of people without diabetes on them for over a month developed high blood sugar ([Li & Cummins, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9116713/)) | Strong |
| Cushing's syndrome raises blood sugar | Diabetes listed as a complication by the NIH ([NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/endocrine-diseases/cushings-syndrome)) | Strong |
| Everyday stress causes lasting blood sugar problems | Hard to separate from sleep, diet and activity | Unclear |
| Coffee after poor sleep raises blood sugar | In a trial of 29 adults, strong coffee after a broken night raised the blood sugar response to a sugary drink ([Smith et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32475359/)) | One small trial |

## What this means day to day

If your energy rises and crashes during the day, cortisol is one possible part of it, but meals, sleep, caffeine and activity matter too. Regular meals that include protein, vegetables and slower carbs are a sensible pattern for steadier energy. This is general healthy-eating advice, not a way to change cortisol.

## When to see a doctor

See a doctor if you have:

- Signs of high blood sugar, such as being very thirsty, urinating a lot, blurred vision or unexplained tiredness
- Diabetes or prediabetes and you're starting steroid medicines
- High blood sugar along with a rounder face, fat between the shoulders, easy bruising or muscle weakness
- Shakiness, sweating or confusion that might be low blood sugar

## FAQ

**Does cortisol raise blood sugar?**
Yes. It increases glucose production in the liver and makes muscle and fat respond less to insulin. That's normal in the short term.

**Can stress cause high blood sugar?**
Short-term stress can raise blood sugar for a while. Whether everyday stress causes lasting problems in healthy people is less clear. If you have diabetes, stress can make blood sugar harder to manage, so talk to your care team.

**Why do steroids raise blood sugar?**
Steroid medicines act like cortisol, so they have the same effect on glucose, often more strongly.
