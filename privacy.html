Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

This site sells a digital guide. The publisher is Kinder Days Press. Checkout runs on Payhip.

This site

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. We do not collect your email on this site. When you buy, checkout is handled by Payhip (payhip.com) and its payment processors (Stripe or PayPal), which receive your name, email, billing country and payment details to complete and deliver your order, under their own privacy policies. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes your IP address to deliver pages securely. If you email support@cortisolparadox.com we use your message only to reply. Data is kept only as long as needed for orders, tax and refunds. You can ask for access, correction or deletion at support@cortisolparadox.com, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Controller: Kinder Days Press, support@cortisolparadox.com.

Purchases

When you buy, you leave this site for a Payhip checkout page. Payhip takes the payment and emails the download. We do not receive your full card number. Payhip handles checkout under its own privacy policy: payhip.com/privacy. Their terms are at payhip.com/terms.

When you click Buy, Payhip's checkout opens and may set cookies needed to process your order, under Payhip's privacy policy.

We may see the email address and order details Payhip shares with the seller, so we can deliver the files and handle a refund.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not buy advertising lists.
  • We do not ask for a phone number, a card number, or a postal address on this site.
