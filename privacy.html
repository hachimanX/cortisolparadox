Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026. This is a plain-language template.

This site sells a digital guide. The publisher is Kinder Days Press. Checkout runs on Payhip. This page says what happens to information when you read the site, join the checklist list, or buy a file.

Purchases

When you buy, you leave this site for a Payhip checkout page. Payhip takes the payment and emails the download. We do not receive your full card number. Payhip’s own privacy policy is at payhip.com/privacy. Their terms are at payhip.com/terms.

We may see the email address and order details Payhip shares with the seller, so we can deliver files and answer a refund email.

The checklist email

The home page has a form for a free 7-Day Calm Cortisol Checklist. That form is not connected yet. Its action is the placeholder #EMAIL-PROVIDER. Submitting it does not add you to a list.

After it is connected, the address you type will be stored by that email provider and used to send the checklist. You can ask for the address to be deleted by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Page counts (GoatCounter)

This site loads GoatCounter, a page counter that is designed to work without cookies. Because that counter does not use cookies, this site does not show a cookie banner for it.

The script is GoatCounter’s standard tag. The site code in it is still the placeholder [GOATCOUNTER-CODE], so the counter is not connected to an account yet. The tag points at https://[GOATCOUNTER-CODE].goatcounter.com/count and loads //gc.zgo.at/count.js.

When a real site code is in place, a page view sends the page address and basic technical details (such as the referring page and the browser) to GoatCounter. We use that only to see how many people open a page. We do not use it to advertise to you. GoatCounter’s site is goatcounter.com.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies for advertising or for the page counter. Payhip may set cookies on their checkout pages. Those cookies are described in Payhip’s policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not buy advertising lists.
  • We do not ask for a phone number, a card number, or a postal address on this site.
